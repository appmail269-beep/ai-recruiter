📲 Install on Android
This app works exactly like a native Android app — no Play Store required. Follow these 5 steps to install it on your home screen.
✅ Supported browsers: Chrome, Edge, Samsung Browser (Android 6+)
⚡ You get: App icon, full-screen, offline support, instant launch
1

Open this page in Chrome

Make sure you're using Google Chrome (or Samsung Browser / Microsoft Edge) on your Android phone. Copy the URL and paste it in Chrome if needed.

2

Tap the ⋮ Three-dot Menu

In Chrome, tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of the browser. A dropdown menu will slide open.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen"

Scroll down in the menu and tap "Add to Home screen". You may also see a blue banner at the bottom of Chrome — tap that too.

💡 May also appear as "Install app"
4

Tap "Add" to Confirm

A dialog shows the app name "AI Jobs". Tap Add or Install to confirm. The icon will be placed on your home screen.

5

Open from Home Screen 🎉

Find the "AI Jobs" icon on your Android home screen. Tap it — the app opens full-screen with no browser bar, just like a native app!

🚀 Launches instantly, works offline too
What you get after installing
📱 Full-screen mode — no browser address bar
⚡ Instant launch from home screen icon
🔄 Offline support — cached content loads always
🔔 Auto-updates when you're online
💾 No Play Store or APK download needed
