📲 Install on Android
Works like a native Android app — no Play Store needed. Follow these 5 steps.
✅ Works on: Chrome, Edge, Samsung Browser (Android 6+)
⚡ You get: App icon, full-screen, offline UI, instant launch
1

Open in Chrome on Android

Make sure you're using Google Chrome. Paste the GitHub Pages URL in Chrome's address bar.

2

Tap the ⋮ Three-dot Menu

Tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen"

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home screen" or the blue Install banner at the bottom.💡 May also say "Install app"

4

Tap "Add" to Confirm

A dialog shows "AI Jobs". Tap Add to place the icon on your home screen.

5

Launch from Home Screen 🎉

Tap the AI Jobs icon — opens full-screen, no browser bar, like a native app!🚀 Works offline too
